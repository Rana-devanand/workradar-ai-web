WorkRadar AI is an AI-powered Work Intelligence Platform.

The platform helps founders, freelancers, consultants, and agencies identify:

• Unanswered emails
• Urgent deadlines
• Revenue opportunities
• Meeting action items

The goal is NOT to create another AI chatbot.

The goal is to create an AI Chief of Staff that continuously tells users what deserves their attention.
